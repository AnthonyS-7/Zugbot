**Seven Bullets** --- *Passive*
At the start of each night, automatically convert any unused Nyaluminum to *Magic Bullet Charges*. If you ever have 7 *Magic Bullet Charges*, you are immediately vanillaized and take 50 damage (this can kill you).

**Pierce the Heart** --- *Night*
Gain 1 *Magic Bullet Charge*. Target a player and do 5 non-critical damage to them for each *Magic Bullet Charge* you have. Your target receives a message based on the amount of charges you have.
