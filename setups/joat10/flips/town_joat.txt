# [color=00ff00] Town JOAT [/color]

You have access to the following three 1-shot night actions during the game:
- Cop: /cop [player] to learn their alignment
- Doctor: /protect [player] to prevent them from dying to night
- Vigilante: /shoot [player] to kill target player

Only one action can be used each night (in other words, these actions cannot be multitasked).

Eliminate all threats to the town, and ensure at least one town-aligned player remains alive.
